Website information

Privacy notice.

A short, transparent notice for this Northstar School OS website preview.

What this preview does

The website is a static marketing preview. In its default configuration, the enquiry form does not send information to our servers or to a third party; it prepares a local copy of your enquiry for you to save or share.

What happens when a contact integration is added

Before a form service is enabled, the site operator should add a full privacy policy naming the form processor, purposes, retention period, lawful basis and contact details. The operator should ensure the form is suitable for its jurisdiction and use.

School and student data

Do not submit student records, medical information or any other sensitive personal information through the marketing enquiry form. Operational school-data practices for a product deployment should be documented separately.

External services

The site loads Google Fonts when internet access is available. For a fully self-hosted deployment, replace those font requests with system fonts or properly licensed self-hosted copies.

Questions

Use the contact page to prepare an enquiry. A published contact address and full operator details need to be configured before the website is promoted as a live lead-capture service.
