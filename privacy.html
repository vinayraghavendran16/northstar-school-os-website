Privacy notice.

How Northstar School OS handles walkthrough enquiries on this website.

Details you provide

If you request a walkthrough, we collect your name, work email, institution and school stage, as well as optional phone number, approximate student count and details of your enquiry. Please do not share student names, medical information, records or any other sensitive personal information in this form.

Why we use this information

We use the information to consider your request, contact you about a walkthrough and coordinate follow-up related to your enquiry. Submitting an enquiry does not subscribe you to a marketing newsletter.

How enquiries are delivered

Northstar's website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The enquiry form submits information to FormSubmit, a third-party service that forwards form submissions by email to the website operator. Review the provider's privacy terms for its processing practices. The provider states its submission archive may be held for 30 days, while email copies may be retained as long as reasonably necessary to follow up.

Sharing and security

We use submitted contact details only to handle requests and do not intentionally sell them. We cannot guarantee absolute security for information transmitted over the internet. Do not submit school operational records or details about individual students through this marketing site.

External resources

The website may request Google Fonts from Google's servers. No school application login is needed to browse this website or submit an enquiry.

Your choices and questions

You can ask Northstar to review, correct or delete your enquiry details by contacting the team through the walkthrough request page or by replying to any email you receive from us. This notice is for the marketing website only; a school deployment of Northstar School OS has separate data handling requirements.
