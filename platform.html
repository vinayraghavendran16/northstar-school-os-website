Built with an honest roadmap

Strong foundations. Thoughtful rollout.

The product already contains substantial operational workflows. That doesn't mean every integration or live-scale readiness item is complete. We believe schools should know the difference.

Core workflows implemented

Student and staff records, academics, attendance, teacher workspace, family access, fees and transport are present in the current application foundation.

Configure before pilot

School-specific setup, real-user access, data migration, permissions and on-campus user testing require careful validation.

Further work ahead

Payment-gateway settlement, configured external notification delivery and robust offline support remain deployment considerations.
